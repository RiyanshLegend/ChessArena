# Magnus Chess AI 🤖♟️

A simple Python-based chess bot that acts as "Magnus AI" by querying the Stockfish engine for the absolute top-rated legal move at every turn.

## Prerequisites

1. **Python 3.8+** installed on your machine.
2. **Stockfish Chess Engine** binary downloaded from the [official Stockfish website](https://stockfishchess.org/download/).
